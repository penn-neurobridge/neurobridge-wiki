  <p class="nb-banner__intro">How the lab works, and the procedures its people follow. The lab is an informatics group in the Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology and Informatics; its data and training come from partner centers across Penn and CHOP, and each center keeps its own procedures. This wiki holds the Lab Manual and points, for each center, to the procedures the lab follows there. It contains no patient identifiers and no credentials.</p>

  <h2>The lab and its centers</h2>
  <p>The Center for Neuroengineering &amp; Therapeutics shares its servers, data and epilepsy pipelines with the lab; the %%SHAREDCOUNT:cnt%% procedures the lab follows there are drawn on the edge between them and open in the CNT manual. The other centers supply cohorts, training and collaborators; their procedures are added as the work produces them.</p>
