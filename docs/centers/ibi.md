# Institute for Biomedical Informatics (IBI)

IBI is the informatics institute of the Perelman School of Medicine. It hosts the informatics faculty, the seminar series and the computing community the lab belongs to. What the lab takes from IBI and contributes to it is described in the Lab Manual, [What this lab is](../lab-manual/what-this-lab-is.md).
