# Center for Neuroengineering & Therapeutics (CNT)

The CNT is home to the Penn Epilepsy Center research groups of Erin Conrad, Kate Davis and Brian Litt. The lab has a touchdown space there and shares its servers (cnt1, cnt-fs, the BSC allocation), its data, its coordinators and the pipelines that take intracranial EEG and imaging from the epilepsy monitoring unit to analysable, de-identified datasets. Most of the lab's epilepsy work runs on this infrastructure.

The CNT keeps its procedures in its own manual, maintained by the CNT associate director: the [CNT procedures manual](cnt:index.md). The lab does not copy those pages. The ones below are the procedures the lab's own people follow there; the rest of the manual (participant-facing coordinator work, shared systems the lab only depends on) is linked from the role routes in [Start here](../lab-manual/start-here.md) where a role needs it.

## Rules that apply on CNT systems

Identified data lives only on cnt1, cnt-fs and the BSC cluster, behind the PMACS VPN; nothing leaves them without the de-identification procedure for its type. Accounts are requested through the CNT's helpdesk procedure under the lab's sponsor and IRB; the data research coordinator files them. The CNT's own staff own the shared systems, so a change to how a shared pipeline works is proposed to them, not made unilaterally.
