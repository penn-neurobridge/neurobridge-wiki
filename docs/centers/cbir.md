# Center for Brain Injury and Repair (CBIR)

CBIR is Penn's traumatic-brain-injury research center. From this partnership come TBI cohorts, their imaging and their clinical trajectories. As with stroke, the lab's data and compute procedures apply unchanged and the imaging procedures will be adapted.
