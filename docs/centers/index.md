# The centers around the lab

NeuroBridge is an informatics lab in the Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology and Informatics. Its data, patients and training come from partner centers across Penn and CHOP, and each partnership brings its own systems and procedures. The lab is the data coordinating center of this network. The pages in this section say, for each center, what it is, what the lab does with it, and which procedures involve it. The [home page](../index.md) draws the same picture as a graph.
