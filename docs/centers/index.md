# The centers the lab works with

The lab's data, patients and training come from partner centers across Penn and CHOP, and each partnership brings its own systems and procedures. The pages in this section say, for each center, what it is, what the lab does with it, and which procedures involve it.

| Center | What the lab does with it | Procedures |
|---|---|---|
| [Center for Neuroengineering & Therapeutics (CNT)](cnt.md) | Joint epilepsy data infrastructure: servers, data, coordinators, pipelines | kept in this wiki and in the CNT manual |
| [Institute for Biomedical Informatics (IBI)](ibi.md) | The lab's informatics institute: faculty, seminars, computing | in preparation |
| [Center for Clinical Epidemiology and Biostatistics (CCEB)](cceb.md) | Training programs several lab members sit in | in preparation |
| [Penn Stroke Center and LCNS](stroke.md) | Stroke and aphasia cohorts, lesion imaging, neuromodulation | in preparation |
| [Center for Brain Injury and Repair (CBIR)](cbir.md) | Traumatic brain injury cohorts and imaging | in preparation |
| [CHOP Informatics](chop.md) | Pediatric informatics collaboration | in preparation |

A procedure that involves a center carries a `center:` line in its front matter (pages shared with the CNT carry `source: cnt`) and is listed on that center's page.
