# The centers around the lab

NeuroBridge is an informatics lab in the Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology and Informatics. Its data, patients and training come from partner centers across Penn and CHOP, and each partnership brings its own systems and procedures. The pages in this section say, for each center, what it is, what the lab does with it, and which of its procedures the lab follows. The [home page](../index.md) draws the same picture as a graph.

| Center | What the lab does with it | Procedures the lab follows |
|---|---|---|
| [Center for Neuroengineering & Therapeutics (CNT)](cnt.md) | Shares servers, data, coordinators and the epilepsy pipelines | %%SHAREDCOUNT:cnt%%, in the CNT manual |
| [Institute for Biomedical Informatics (IBI)](ibi.md) | The lab's informatics institute: faculty, seminars, computing | none yet |
| [Center for Clinical Epidemiology and Biostatistics (CCEB)](cceb.md) | Training programs several lab members sit in | none yet |
| [Penn Stroke Center and LCNS](stroke.md) | Stroke and aphasia cohorts, lesion imaging, neuromodulation | none yet |
| [Center for Brain Injury and Repair (CBIR)](cbir.md) | Traumatic brain injury cohorts and imaging | none yet |
| [CHOP Informatics](chop.md) | Pediatric informatics collaboration | none yet |

When a collaboration produces a procedure the lab's own people run, it is written in this wiki under the theme it belongs to (data, compute, imaging, electrophysiology, REDCap, operations) with a `center:` line in its front matter, and it appears on that center's page and on the graph.
