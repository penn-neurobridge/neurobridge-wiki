# 5. The six themes at a glance

One paragraph per theme and the three pages to read first.

## [Data](../data/index.md)

What the lab holds and how it is organized, stored, moved, shared, archived and de-identified. The site inventory and the BIDS specification are the lab's own; the SOPs were migrated. **Read first:** Data Storage Locations · Data Structure version 1.0 · Pennsieve Data Access Rules.

## [Compute](../compute/index.md)

Two worlds: SEAS/CETS (Borel, Leif, Pioneer, Finkel under SLURM) and PMACS/PSOM (cnt1, cnt-fs, BSC, cntgpu1), plus AWS. Identified data lives only on the PMACS side. **Read first:** Overview of CNT Systems · VDI, cnt-fs and cnt1 · Submitting Jobs with SLURM.

## [Imaging](../imaging/index.md)

The MRI/CT lifecycle from booking a research scan to a reconstructed electrode map. **Read first:** Visit Checklist: 3T Study · Flywheel → cnt-fs · Electrode Reconstruction: Prep & Software.

## [Electrophysiology](../electrophysiology/index.md)

The EEG/iEEG lifecycle from the EMU to [ieeg.org](http://ieeg.org) — the lab's deepest documentation. **Read first:** sEEG Phase II Processing: Overview · Setup for Processing · Processing for [ieeg.org](http://ieeg.org).

## [REDCap & Clinical Metadata](../redcap/index.md)

The clinical-variable layer that joins to every dataset. The thinnest theme today and the one that will grow. **Read first:** Surgical Outcomes REDCap Project · RADAR Pull → REDCap Entry · REDCap Tips.

## [Operations](../operations/index.md)

Getting set up (every access request, onboarding) and running studies (IRB, consent, scheduling, reimbursement, neuropsych testing). **Read first:** CRC Access Checklist · Onboarding & Offboarding Checklist · Consent Signing Checklist.
